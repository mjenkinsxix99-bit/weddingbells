Travel & Stay

We're getting married at The Springs in Lake Conroe, just north of Houston, Texas. Making a weekend of it? Here's what you'll need to plan your trip — where to stay, how to get here, and a few local favorites.

While You're Here

Things to Do

❦ On the Lake

Lake Conroe is made for a slow afternoon — waterfront dining, boat rentals, and sunsets over the water. We'll share our favorite spots.

❦ Nearby Towns

Historic Montgomery and downtown Conroe are close by, with shops, coffee, and good food. A recommendations list is on the way.

❦ For the Adventurers

Ask us about Garden of the Gods — it holds a very special place in our story. 💍

② Flying In

The closest major airport is Houston — George Bush Intercontinental (IAH), roughly 45–60 minutes from Lake Conroe. William P. Hobby (HOU) is another option. A rental car is the easiest way to reach the venue.
