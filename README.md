# 💍 Sophia & James — Wedding Website

A simple, elegant wedding website built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
No frameworks, no build step — just open it and it works.

> **Note:** The names, date, venue, story, and photos are all *placeholders* right
> now so we could get a real page on screen. We'll swap in the real details together.

## 📄 The pages

| File           | What it is                                                        |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `index.html`   | Home — the couple, the date, a live countdown, ceremony & reception details, and the day's schedule. |
| `story.html`   | Our Story — a timeline of how they met, plus a photo gallery.     |
| `travel.html`  | Travel & Registry — hotels, shuttle info, local things to do, and gift links. |

## 👀 How to see it

Just double-click `index.html` and it opens in your web browser. That's it.

## ✏️ What to change (the important bits)

Everything personal lives in a few obvious spots:

1. **The names, date, venue, and story text** — these are written right into the
   three `.html` files. Tell me the real details and I'll replace them everywhere.
2. **The countdown date** — one line at the top of `js/main.js`, clearly marked
   `✏️ EDIT ME`.
3. **The colors** — all defined once at the top of `css/styles.css` under
   `--- Palette ---`. Change the vibe by changing those.
4. **The photos** — drop real images into the `assets/` folder and we'll point the
   gallery at them (they currently show a "Photo coming soon" placeholder).
